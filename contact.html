Aam Sawaalaat (FAQs)

Sukkur-Karachi safar ke hawalay se zaroori maloomat

Aam tor par bus ko 7 se 8 ghantay lagte hain, depend karta hai ke konsa route liya gaya hai aur kitne stops hain. Motorway (M-5 aur M-9) use karne se waqt kam lagta hai.
Standard coaches (jaise Al-Saif ya deegar local coaches) ka kiraya taqreeban PKR 1,500 se 1,800 ke darmian hota hai.
Raat ka safar kaafi popular aur mehfooz hai kyun ke aap subah Karachi pohanch jatay hain. Faisal Movers aur Daewoo ki night buses bohat aaram-deh hain.
Haan, takreeban tamam achi companies single ladies ke liye agay ki seats mehfooz rakhti hain. Aap ticket book karte waqt request kar sakte hain.
Aap Bookme.pk, SastaTicket ya company ki apni app se online tickets easily book kar sakte hain. Payment JazzCash, EasyPaisa ya card se ho sakti hai.
Ziada tar barri bus companies ke terminals Rohri Bypass aur Military Road ke ird gird waqay hain.
Aam tor par ek musafir ko 20 se 30 kg tak saman le janay ki ijazat hoti hai. Izafi saman par extra charges lag sakte hain.
Premium services jaise Daewoo Gold ya Faisal Movers mein safar ke doran refreshments (snacks aur drinks) diye jatay hain. Iske ilawa ek rest stop zaroor hota hai jahan aap khana kha sakte hain.
